Instructions for RCSB PDB
  1. Open the RCSB PDB link
  2. Query the RCSB PDB using protein name and species of interest
  3. Identify protein structure of interest and open PDB from RCSB PDB as a .txt file and copy the PDB text
  4. In the "Information for TM-align" section of SeqAPASS, paste the PDB text and fill in available information about the structure
  5. Select "Push to TM-align" to submit the externally generated structure to TM-align
